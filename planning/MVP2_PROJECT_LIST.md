# MVP 2 Project List

Last updated: 2026-08-11

## Private photos and PDF evidence

Status: `placeholder-approved-deferred-to-mvp2`

Private photos and PDFs are not required for the current MVP. Trainers may continue to enter and receive the approved numeric information without uploading private evidence.

The MVP 2 placeholder retains future work for optional supporting evidence attached to the exact horse and test, including:

- meter or equipment photos
- sample labels or test-setup photos
- veterinarian or laboratory PDF reports
- other approved operational evidence

Before this capability can be activated, MVP 2 must define and prove allowed file types, size and count limits, malware scanning or sanitisation, metadata/privacy handling, horse/test access controls, cross-horse and cross-stable denial, retention, deletion, restoration, legal hold, purge, download expiry, cleanup and Storage-object recovery.

OCR, colour analysis and meter-screen interpretation remain separate future enhancements. No automatic extraction may become authoritative without trainer confirmation.

Sprint 023Q is reserved as the future MVP 2 planning placeholder for this outcome. It is not the next current-MVP sprint and does not authorize implementation, Storage changes, uploads, private-file collection, deployment or any external action.
