## Stop conditions

Stop and clean exact-owned state if:

- source, Preview, Supabase project/region, ledger or initial zero differs;
- ceiling/topology/ownership exceeds two stables, four horses or three identities;
- protected values or real/unexpected data appear;
- Stable B influences writer lists, counts, ordering, summaries or actions;
- direct cross-stable route/write/evidence denial fails;
- RLS, revocation, integrity, evidence safety or context isolation fails;
- schema/RLS/permission/dependency/new-authority change is required;
- Production/Stripe/non-owned state would be touched;
- exact cleanup cannot be proven.
