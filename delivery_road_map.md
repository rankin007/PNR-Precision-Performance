## Purpose and authority

This roadmap answers four questions:

1. What outcome must each remaining sprint deliver?
2. Which canonical acceptance gaps does it close?
3. What authority or evidence must exist before that sprint can succeed?
4. What remains after the sprint closes?

When records disagree, current repository and executable evidence win over prose. The latest accepted decisions, the canonical Done definition and the Final Product Acceptance Matrix govern the finish line. The Architect must reconcile any conflict as a visible edit; no sprint may silently lower the Done standard.

Roadmap rows do not authorize implementation, remote mutation, provider action, deployment, migration, commit, push or Production change.

## Mechanical Done gate

Precision Performance may be called **Done** only when all of the following are true:

- Every mandatory row in `planning/FINAL_PRODUCT_ACCEPTANCE_MATRIX.md` is `passed`, or `passed-with-accepted-limitation` backed by an explicit owner decision that is consistent with the canonical Done definition.
- No mandatory row remains `authority-required`, `not-proven` or `not-done`.
- Any capability removed or deferred from the finish line has an explicit accountable-owner decision and a visible amendment to the Done definition and acceptance matrix. Silence, delay or missing input is not acceptance.
- A real trainer completes the supported mobile journey, including a timed test submission, without protected credentials or private data entering repository or chat evidence.
- Approved results, explanations, recommendations and trends are understandable and do not imply diagnosis, guaranteed performance or replacement of veterinary judgment.
- Trainer, owner, veterinary/staff and administrator journeys pass their approved permission boundaries.
- Required evidence files are available, private, lifecycle-governed and denied across the wrong horse/stable boundary; or the Done definition is explicitly amended not to require them.
- Production source, migration state, environment, routing, monitoring, rollback and backup/restoration evidence are reconciled.
- Offering, onboarding, support, privacy and incident ownership are named and rehearsed.
- The final exact-source Production candidate passes the complete acceptance matrix and the final records truthfully state `Done`.
